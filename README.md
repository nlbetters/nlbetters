### 🔬 Research

**Bayesian Fourier Neural Operators for uncertainty quantification**
*Summer Undergraduate Research Experience, Ralph O'Connor Sustainable Energy Institute (ROSEI), Johns Hopkins University. Shields Uncertainty Research Group.*

I studied how Bayesian Fourier Neural Operators can attach uncertainty estimates to neural network predictions, for energy forecasting and grid modeling. I ran experiments comparing deterministic and Bayesian models and tuned hyperparameters in PyTorch.

- Wrote [`BayesianConv3d`](https://github.com/SURGroup/UQpy/blob/main/src/UQpy/scientific_machine_learning/layers/BayesianConv3d.py), a Bayesian 3D convolution layer that's now part of the open-source [UQpy](https://github.com/SURGroup/UQpy) library
- [Research poster (PDF)](research/bfno-poster.pdf)
- Mentored by Connor Krill, with Prof. Michael Shields
